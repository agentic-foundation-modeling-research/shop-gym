# homepage_sections

## Site shell
- Announcement bar fixed to the top with a single rotating message.
- Horizontal header with a mega menu (two columns) and right-aligned
  account, search, and cart icons.
- Footer split into four link groups plus a newsletter form.

## Homepage sections
1. Hero carousel (three slides, autoplay, dot navigation).
2. Featured collection grid (eight tiles).
3. Promotional banner with a single call-to-action button.
4. Customer testimonials (three quotes, swipeable).
5. Newsletter sign-up.

A modal popup appears on the first visit advertising the newsletter.
