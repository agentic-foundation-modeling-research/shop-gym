You are looking at a product detail page (PDP) of an online store.

Decide whether the page exposes a clearly-identifiable product title —
the name of the single product this page is about. The title is
typically the most prominent heading on the page.

Treat as **present** when there is exactly one prominent name that
identifies the product. Treat as **not present** when the page shows
only a category heading, no heading at all, or competing names of
equal prominence (e.g. several recommended products).

Set `name_used` to the product title text. Set `evidence_locator` to a
short hint (e.g. "h1 heading", "top-of-page title region").
