## Focus hint

The person who runs this exploration gave a focus: "{{FOCUS}}". The
focus names one part of the site, for example a department or a
category. It is a hint for your choice of pages, not a filter.

- Pick the listing pages, product pages and search queries inside the
  focus, for your own browse and for the task briefs. When a rule above
  picks one of these pages, pick it inside the focus. The focus does
  not change your browse budget.
- Keep every canonical task, also the site-wide ones (header, footer,
  cart, checkout entry, account entries, info pages, floating widgets).
  A task may open a page outside the focus when it needs that page.
- The executor sees only its brief. Write the focus into the brief of
  each task that opens a listing page, a product page or a search, for
  example `open a "{{FOCUS}}" listing page; exercise filters + sort + pagination`.
- If no part of the site matches the focus, plan as if there were no
  focus, and say so in the plan summary paragraph.
