# Code of Conduct

ShopGym is intended to be a respectful, collaborative project for people
building and evaluating shopping agents.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, discussions, and
  reviews.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the impact of your words and
  actions.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, tradeoffs, and maintainable
  outcomes.
- Give and receive feedback with care, especially when contributors are new to
  the project or the domain.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissive comments about a person's identity,
  background, or experience level.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would make reasonable contributors feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting

Report code of conduct concerns to `opensource@shopify.com`. Reports should
include enough context for maintainers to understand what happened, such as
links to public comments, screenshots, or relevant dates.

Maintainers will review reports privately, take proportionate action, and avoid
retaliation against people who report concerns in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, block
participants, or take other actions needed to protect the project community.
Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent removal from project
spaces.
